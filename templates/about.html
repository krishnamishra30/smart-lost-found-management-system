About Smart Lost & Found

Smart Lost & Found is a campus-focused platform designed to make reporting, searching, matching, claiming, and recovering lost and found items simple, organized, and efficient.

Our System

The system provides a centralized platform where students and staff can report lost items, report found items, search available reports, and track the recovery process from one place.

Our Goal

Our goal is to reduce the difficulty of finding lost belongings on campus by connecting lost and found reports and providing a structured recovery process.

Why Smart Lost & Found?

Traditional lost-and-found processes can be difficult to manage because information may be scattered across different people or locations. This platform brings reports into one centralized system and uses smart matching based on item information such as category, name, description, location, and color to help identify possible matches.

Key Features

Everything needed for an organized campus recovery process.

Secure Authentication

User registration and login provide controlled access to the platform.

Lost & Found Reports

Users can submit detailed lost and found item reports with important item information.

Search & Filtering

Search reports using keywords, categories, report type, and locations.

Smart Matching

The system compares lost and found information to identify possible matches.

Claim Management

Users can submit claims and administrators can review, approve, or reject them.

Status Tracking

Users can track reports and claims throughout the recovery process.
