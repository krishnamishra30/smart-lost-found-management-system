Help & Support

Find quick answers to common questions and learn how to use the Smart Lost & Found Management System.

How do I report a lost item?

Login to your account, open Report Lost Item, enter the required item details and submit the report.

How do I report a found item?

Login to your account, select Report Found Item, provide the found item's details and submit the report.

How does Smart Matching work?

The system compares details such as category, item name, description, location and color to identify possible matches between lost and found reports.

How do I claim an item?

Open Smart Matches, select a suitable found item and choose Claim This Item. Explain why the item belongs to you and submit the claim.

Who verifies claims?

An authorized administrator reviews submitted claims and can approve or reject them after checking the provided information.

Where can I track my status?

You can monitor your reports from My Reports, your claims from My Claims, or view the combined recovery information from Track & Recover.

How can I search for an item?

Open the Search page and use keywords, category, item type or location to narrow down the available lost and found reports.

What happens after a claim is submitted?

Your claim remains in a pending state until an authorized administrator reviews it. The result can be monitored through My Claims and Track & Recover.
