How Smart Lost & Found Works

From reporting an item to successful recovery, the platform provides a simple and centralized process for managing lost and found belongings on campus.

1

Report

Report a lost or found item by providing important details such as category, description, location, date and other identifying information.

2

Smart Matching

The system compares item information and identifies possible matches between lost and found reports.

3

Claim

If a found item appears to match your lost item, submit a claim with an explanation for verification.

4

Verification

An authorized administrator reviews the claim and verifies the information before approving or rejecting it.

5

Recovery

After successful verification, the item status can be updated and the recovery process can be tracked.

Complete Recovery Flow
Register / Login → Report → Smart Match → Claim → Admin Verification → Returned
Purpose: Keep campus lost-and-found information centralized instead of depending on notice boards, messaging groups or verbal communication.

What the System Provides

Key features that support the complete lost-and-found recovery process.

User Authentication Secure registration and login for accessing the platform.
Lost & Found Reports Submit detailed lost and found item reports.
Search & Filtering Find relevant reports using search and filtering options.
Smart Matching Compare item information to identify possible matches.
Claim Management Submit and monitor claims through administrator verification.
Status Tracking Track report and claim status through the recovery process.
