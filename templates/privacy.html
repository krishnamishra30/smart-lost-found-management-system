Privacy Policy

Learn how information is handled within the Smart Lost & Found Management System.

About This Policy

This project is designed as a campus lost-and-found management system. This Privacy Policy explains the intended handling of information used by the system for reporting, searching, matching, claiming, and tracking lost and found items.

Information We Store

Account details and item-report information are stored to provide reporting, smart matching, claim management, and status tracking functionality. The stored information may include user account details and information related to reported lost and found items.

Password Security

Website passwords are stored using password hashing rather than plain-text storage. This helps protect user passwords from being directly exposed in the database.

Access Control

Administrative functions are restricted to accounts assigned the administrator role. Regular users cannot directly access administrator-only management functions through the normal application interface.

Item Information

Item descriptions, categories, locations, dates, colors, images, and identification details may be used for searching, smart matching, claim verification, and recovery of lost property.

System Usage

Information submitted through the system is used to provide the application's core features, including lost-item reporting, found-item reporting, search, smart matching, claim management, and recovery tracking.

Academic Project Use

This policy describes the intended information handling for the academic Smart Lost & Found Management System. Additional privacy, security, retention, and institutional compliance requirements should be added before deploying the system for real-world institutional use.

Important: This website is developed as an academic project. The privacy practices described here represent the intended behavior of the project and may require further enhancement for production deployment.
